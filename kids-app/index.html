📖 How to use

  1. It starts loaded: the app comes preloaded with a starter set of learning videos — ABCs, first words, colors, numbers, a little Spanish, and songs. You can take any of them out, or clear them all and build your own list.
  2. Add videos — paste a YouTube link, or search by name and tap Add.
  3. Put them in order — use the ↑ ↓ buttons so they play the way you want.
  4. Tap Play. The videos play one after another, then start over.
  5. Tap “Lock the screen” and set a 4-number PIN only you know.
  6. Hand the phone over. Little hands can tap all they want — nothing will pause, skip, or wander off.
  7. To unlock: press and hold the top-right corner of the screen for 3 seconds, then enter your PIN.

Good to know (the honest part)

The lock covers this app's screen. The phone's own buttons, a swipe down from the very top (the notification shade), and incoming calls can still interrupt — no web page can block those.

For a total lockdown, add the phone's built-in feature too: iPhone — Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access, turn it on, then triple-click the side button inside the app. Android — Settings → Security → App pinning, turn it on, then pin this app from the recent-apps screen.

Forgot your PIN? Hold the corner for 3 seconds, tap Forgot PIN?, and type the word we ask for — that clears the PIN (your playlist stays). Clearing this site's data in your browser settings also removes the PIN, but the playlist goes with it.

Pick videos together with your kids, and only add channels you trust — every video here is one you chose.
