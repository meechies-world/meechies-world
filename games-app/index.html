ABOUT THE GAMES

Coin Jackpot is the coin floor: eight fast games played with Meechie's World coins, all decided by the server and paid out of one shared prize pool that fills with lost bets (minus a 10% house fee) and the owner's own seed coins. Bets start at 5 coins, and the pool sets each game's biggest bet, so a win can always be paid. Sign in free to play.

  • Coin Flip — call heads or tails; a right call pays 1.9×
  • High Card — your card against the dealer's; higher wins 1.9×, a tie refunds your bet
  • Lucky Dice — call two dice under 7, exactly 7 (5.4×), or over 7
  • Gold Slots — three reels; any pair pays, three crowns pay 25×
  • Meechie Race — four dice run your crown down a 24-space board; 21+ pays 5×
  • Wheel of Gold — a 12-segment wheel; the gold segment pays 4.5×
  • Gold Drop — a ball bounces through pegs into 8 slots; the edges pay 8×
  • Triple Flip — three coins at once; all heads or all tails pays 7×

There is also a player-vs-player pot board: post coins on heads or tails and another member takes the other side — winner takes the pot, minus the fee. Coins come from the free daily check-in, the 100-coin welcome drop, wins, or packs (100 coins for $1 up to 6,250 for $50), and they are spent on Jackpot bets and on gifts — gift a member and they earn 40% of the value as real cash they can cash out from $10.

Gold Rush Arena is a free real-time battle game: move with WASD or arrows (touch on a phone), aim and shoot, outlast the shrinking gold circle, respawn and go again. Members battle live; bots fill in when you are alone. Predictions is the free points game: everyone starts with 1,000 points, Meechie posts the questions, and winners split the pot — points have no cash value, ever. Full rules for every game are on the Games page in the app.
