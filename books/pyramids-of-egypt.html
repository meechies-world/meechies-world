About the book

Almost everyone who sees the Great Pyramid asks the same questions. How was it built? What was it for? And who was really behind it? This book answers all three with evidence: the quarries, the tools, the harbor, the bakeries, the workers' tombs, and a logbook written on papyrus by a boat foreman named Merer more than 4,500 years ago.

It also gives a fair hearing to the popular theories, including the "Giza Power Plant," ancient astronauts, the Orion correlation, and "pyramid power," and sets each claim beside the evidence, so you can judge for yourself.

Inside the book

How they were built

Surveying, quarrying, sledges and wet sand, boats, ramps, and the arithmetic of the work, worked out step by step.

Who built them

Not slaves: a fed, housed, and honored workforce, the town they lived in, and a day on the plateau reconstructed from the evidence.

What they were for

Kingship, the afterlife, the Pyramid Texts, and the temples, boats, and priests of each pyramid complex.

Myths, tested

Power plants, aliens, secret numbers, and lost civilizations: what is claimed, where it came from, and what the evidence says.

The frontier

Cosmic-ray scans, a buried branch of the Nile, new voids in Menkaure's pyramid, and how to read a pyramid headline.

Quick reference

A timeline, tables of the major pyramids, a glossary, and a reading list for going deeper.
