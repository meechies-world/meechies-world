Legal Help

Representing yourself (pro se)? Build your case, and let Meechie's AI draft your motion, petition or complaint with real case law, then check every citation before you file.

⚖️ Legal document preparation, not legal advice. Meechie's World isn't a law firm and the AI isn't a lawyer. You review, sign and file your own papers. Always read the cases you cite, check your court's local rules and deadlines, and talk to a lawyer or legal aid if you can. Your draft stays on this device unless you choose to request paid document preparation. If you submit a request, the case details you include are sent privately to Meechie for that purpose.

❓ Ask a legal question

How does bail work? What's a 2255? How do I serve the other side? The AI explains in plain words and looks up real cases.

⏰ Common deadlines (federal court)

Missing a deadline can end your case. These are the usual federal limits. State courts set their own, so look up your state's rules or ask the clerk. Count calendar days, and check whether your court counts weekends and holidays.

14 daysNotice of appeal in a criminal case, after the judgment Fed. R. App. P. 4(b)(1)(A)
30 daysNotice of appeal in a civil case, after the judgment (60 days if the U.S. is a party) Fed. R. App. P. 4(a)(1)
21 daysAnswer a federal civil complaint after you're served Fed. R. Civ. P. 12(a)(1)(A)(i)
28 daysMotion to alter or amend a civil judgment, or for a new civil trial Fed. R. Civ. P. 59(b), (e)
14 daysMotion for a new trial in a criminal case, unless it's based on newly discovered evidence (3 years) Fed. R. Crim. P. 33(b)
1 yearFederal prisoner's § 2255 motion, usually counted from when the conviction became final 28 U.S.C. § 2255(f)
1 yearState prisoner's federal habeas petition, with time paused while a proper state petition is pending 28 U.S.C. § 2244(d)
14 daysObjections to a magistrate judge's report and recommendation Fed. R. Civ. P. 72(b)(2); 28 U.S.C. § 636(b)(1)
