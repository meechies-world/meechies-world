MW TV Sponsors

Paid commercials from /promote. Nothing airs until you approve it here. Approving puts the spot into the MW TV break rotation on the station keeper's next pass (about a minute) — and its paid week starts then, not when they paid. Expired spots stop on their own.

Rights: sponsors submit their own creative and are responsible for the rights to their own ad — footage, music, logos, all of it. If a spot looks like it uses somebody else's music or brand, reject it and tell them why.
