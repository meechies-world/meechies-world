How a paid MW TV commercial works

  1. 1. Pick your tier — Free Week, Starter ($25/week) or Featured ($50/week — about double the rotation).
  2. 2. Upload your commercial — your video file goes straight into the station's media library. No spot yet? See the production note in the form — we can make it for you.
  3. 3. Pay (paid tiers) — one secure Stripe payment for the week. The Free Week skips this step entirely.
  4. 4. Malik approves it — every spot is reviewed by hand before it airs. Approved spots enter the MW TV break rotation — and your audio cut rides the radio spot lane too, at song breaks.
  5. 5. Your plays are counted — the station counts every time your spot is served, and we can show you the tally. That's your proof.

Your commercial is your own creative: by submitting it you confirm you hold the rights to everything in it — footage, music, logos, all of it.
